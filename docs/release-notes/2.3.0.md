**BentoBar 2.3**: Next meeting gets your meetings right, and you choose which events count.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

**Optional online items.** Only **Weather** and **Air quality & UV** (Open-Meteo, no key), **Exchange rates** (Frankfurter, no key), **Flight** (AirLabs, with a free key of your own) and **US stocks** (Finnhub, with a free key of your own) go online, and only after you set them up; **Setup › Online services** switches each off again. **About › Privacy** in the app says exactly what is sent and when.

The full guide, screenshots and privacy notes are in the [README](https://github.com/Meridian11Labs/bentobar#readme).

### What's new
- **Only your meetings.** A colleague's meeting on a shared calendar no longer shows as yours unless you are invited
  to it. A booked room is not a guest, so a block with only you and a room is no meeting. A canceled event is gone,
  from Next meeting and from the month view. An event marked free is no meeting.
- **A meeting inside a long one.** During a workshop, the meeting that starts in the middle of it shows from your
  "Show when" minutes before it starts, in the bar, the Live Update chip and Live activity, and the workshop comes
  back after it. Back-to-back meetings are as before: the one that is on stays until it ends.
- **Calendar events, in Setup.** A new card says which events count as meetings, for every Next meeting item, the
  chip and Live activity. **Calendars**: untick a calendar and its events never count. **Events with only you**:
  never, with a call link (as before), or always. **Invitations you haven't answered**: count them or not. **Events
  marked as free**: count them or not. **Leave out titles with**: words or phrases, such as "Hold" or "Commute",
  found as whole words, up to 20 of up to 80 characters. These choices stay on this device.
- **More for Next meeting.** **In the bar** can show the time alone ("in 12m", "14:30") instead of the title and
  the time, and Live activity then shows no title either. **Looks ahead: Any day** shows your next meeting when there is none left today ("Standup Tue 10:00"),
  and its menu lists that day's meetings.
- **Meetings in the month view.** Date and calendar's month lists every event of a day, meetings too, with Join and
  Directions. One meeting on two of your calendars counts once in Next meeting and the chip, and a meeting you
  declined on your own calendar no longer counts through its copy on a shared one.
- **From 2.2.1**, for everyone coming from 2.2.0: the CPU item reads the load your device really has (#53), and the
  Network menu shows the connection's addresses (#47).

### Upgrading from 2.2
- Events marked as free and blocks with only you and a booked room no longer count as meetings. **Setup › Calendar
  events › Events marked as free** brings free events back.
- A meeting you make on a shared or secondary calendar without inviting yourself is not counted as yours: the
  calendar doesn't say who made an event, so it can't be told from a colleague's. Add yourself as a guest and it
  counts.
- The choices in **Calendar events** are per device. They are no part of your layout: Copy settings, a backup and a
  new device don't carry them, and you set them again on another Googlebook.
- Nothing else of your layout changes, and nothing new is asked for.
