**BentoBar 1.6**: a clipboard and scratchpad, external displays, air quality and UV, and exchange rates in the bar.

### Install
1. Download **BentoBar.apk** below on your Googlebook and open it (allow Chrome or Files to install apps if asked).
2. Open BentoBar. It says what its accessibility service does and asks whether you agree; **Agree** opens Android's Accessibility settings. The first time, Android marks the switch as a *restricted setting*: tap it once, then in BentoBar tap **Setup** › **App info** › **⋮** › **Allow restricted settings**, and turn BentoBar on again.

### What's new
- **Clipboard and scratchpad.** Keep up to eight pinned snippets and your recent session clips one click away. Clicking a snippet copies it. Recent clips stay in memory only.
- **External displays.** Shows the resolution and refresh rate (`4K 60Hz`, `1440p 144Hz`) when an external monitor is plugged in, with each connected screen's resolution, refresh rate and HDR status in the menu, plus a **Keep awake while connected** switch for docked setups.
- **Air quality and UV.** US or European AQI and UV index from Open-Meteo for your city or **My location**, with PM2.5, PM10, ozone and daily peak UV in the menu, and a threshold to pop out when smoke, smog or UV is high.
- **Exchange rates.** Follow up to six currency pairs (`EUR/USD`, `USD/JPY`, `GBP/USD`, and 30+ currencies) rotating in the bar, with daily percent moves and an inline currency converter in the menu. Reference rates come from the European Central Bank via Frankfurter (no API key needed).
